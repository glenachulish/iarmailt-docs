# Privacy Policy: Mullach

*Last updated: 10 November 2026*

Mullach is made by the developer named on its App Store page. It has no account
and no server of its own. The only thing that reaches the developer is feedback
you choose to send.

It does use the internet, because forecasts and web pages have to come from
somewhere. This page says exactly what leaves your device, who receives it, and
what stays put.

## What the app stores, and where

Your hill diary (the hills you log, dates, notes and trips), the days you plan,
your notes on Library topics and sections, the photos, videos and voice notes you
attach, your map pins, your favourite webcams and any webcams you add are kept in
a database inside the app's own container on your device.

There is no Mullach account and no sign-in. Nobody, including the developer, can
see any of this. Kit list ticks, recent places and your settings stay on the device.

## iCloud

Those same items also go to **your own iCloud account**, so your diary is the
same on your iPhone, your iPad and your Mac. This happens automatically when you
are signed in to iCloud on the device; there is nothing to switch on.

They go into your account's *private* database. Apple encrypts it, the developer
has no access to it and no way to ask for access, and no other user of Mullach
can reach it.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, the app keeps working and everything stays
on that device.

Hills you save for use without a signal are different: those downloads (a
forecast snapshot and a map picture) stay on the device that made them and are
not sent to iCloud. You can delete them one at a time or all together in the app.

A hill day you plan saves its own downloads in the same way as you build it: a
forecast and map picture for each hill, and a map picture of the whole day. You can
delete a plan's downloads from the plan, and they go if you delete the plan.

If you have iCloud Backup switched on, your device may also include the app's
data in your own encrypted backup. Both that and the sync above are Apple's
services under your own Apple Account, covered by Apple's privacy policy rather
than this one.

## Your location

If you allow it, the map shows where you are and can centre on you. Mullach
does not record your location, keep a history of it, or send it anywhere on its
own.

When you ask for a forecast for a point on the map, the coordinates of that
point are sent to the forecast service you have chosen (see below). If the point
happens to be where you are standing, that is your location. You can allow or
refuse location in your device's Settings at any time, and the app works without it.

## Where the internet is used

**Forecasts.** When you open a forecast, or save a hill for offline use, the
coordinates of that point are sent to **MET Norway** (api.met.no) or to **Apple
Weather**, whichever you have chosen on the forecast screen. Like any web
request, this shows the service your device's IP address. Mullach adds no name,
account or identifier. MET Norway and Apple each handle what they receive under
their own terms and privacy policies.

**Maps and search.** The map, its place search and the map pictures saved with
offline hills, day plans and diary books come from **Apple Maps**, under Apple's
privacy policy. If you type a starting point into a plan, that text goes to Apple
Maps to find the place for the map picture.

**Web pages and webcams.** Forecasts from MWIS, the Met Office and the Scottish
Avalanche Information Service, route pages on Walkhighlands and UKHillwalking,
crag pages on UKClimbing, hill details, mountain-forecast.com, pressure charts and
your webcams are loaded from their publishers. Most open inside a Mullach window, so for as
long as you are looking at one you are on that publisher's website, and what that
site collects, including cookies and advertising, is theirs to describe. Mullach
adds nothing about you to the request. Where it does not know a hill's page on
UKHillwalking it opens a web search on DuckDuckGo instead.

**Library videos.** A video plays in the official YouTube player. When you start
one, YouTube receives the request and handles it under Google's privacy policy.

**Outdooractive and OS Maps.** These links open outside Mullach, in the
Outdooractive or OS Maps app if you have it, otherwise your browser. The address
contains the summit's coordinates, not yours.

If you choose **Save to my notes** on a web page, a copy of that page is kept in
your notebook (and your iCloud), and nothing further is sent.

## Feedback you choose to send

**Send feedback** and **Suggest a correction** send a note to the developer, only
when you press Send.

What is sent: your message, your name if you type one, the category you pick, the
hill, crag or Library page it is about with the details shown on that page, and
the app's name and version. iCloud also attaches a random identifier for your
account in this app. It does not show the developer your Apple Account or your
email address.

Where it goes: the note is saved in the app's public iCloud database, which is
separate from your private one. Mullach does not show notes to other people, but
that database doesn't have the private database's protections, so please don't
put anything sensitive in a note. If iCloud isn't available, your email app opens
instead, and your email address is then visible to the developer as with any email.

What it is for: reading and answering suggestions, and improving the app, with
your name used only to credit you if you asked for that by giving it. It isn't
shared with anyone. Notes are kept for as long as they are useful for that. To
have one removed, email the address below and say what it said.

Your name is remembered on your device so you don't have to type it again.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting sent anywhere.
- No advertising of its own, and no advertising identifier.
- No tracking, and no sharing of anything with any other company.
- No accounts and no sign-in.
- No third-party SDKs.

## Photos, video and voice notes

Photos and videos are added through the system photo picker, which gives
Mullach only the items you choose and not your library. Pictures you add to a plan
from Files come only from the files you choose. The microphone is used
only while you are recording a voice note. Everything you add is compressed and
kept in your notebook, and in your iCloud as above.

## The diagnostic log

Mullach keeps a small text log on your device of technical events, such as
screens opened and errors, to help find faults. It is never sent anywhere, and it
goes when you delete the app.

## Files you share

Exporting your diary as a CSV file or a hills book as a PDF, and sharing a plan,
a kit list or a tick list, all use the standard share sheet, so the file goes wherever you choose to send it and nowhere else.
The app does not send it anywhere itself.

## Children

Mullach has no accounts, no messaging, no user-generated content visible to
anyone else, and no purchases. The only thing it can send to the developer is
feedback someone chooses to send, as described above. The web pages it can open
belong to other publishers.

## Changes

If any of this changes, the policy will be updated before the change ships, and
the date at the top will say when. A policy that describes some other version of
the app is a false statement about this one.
